Execution boundary

This site displays results. It does not launch training or connect bank clients. Flower Chat is a separate interface.

Privacy boundary

Separate processes on one laptop are not secure isolation. Model updates can still leak information; federated learning alone does not establish privacy.

Evidence boundary

Synthetic-data findings do not establish real-world AML performance, regulatory compliance, or production readiness.
